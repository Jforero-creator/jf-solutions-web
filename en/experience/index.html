Method

One method, from the floor to the evidence

  1. On-the-floor assessment

    We start where the work happens: receiving, lines, storage and records.

  2. Risk-based priorities

    Findings are ranked by risk so effort goes where it matters first.

  3. Implementation with owners

    Each action has a clear owner and a date.

  4. Verification with evidence

    Effectiveness is checked with records, not assumptions.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find quality and food safety consulting in New Jersey and New York?

JF Solutions provides practical consulting support for quality control, food safety, industrial traceability and process improvement for manufacturers and industrial operations serving New Jersey and New York.

How can a manufacturer prepare for FSMA 204?

JF Solutions can support a readiness assessment, process mapping, traceability gap identification and corrective action planning. Applicability and compliance requirements must be assessed for each operation.

Can JF Solutions help investigate recurring nonconformities?

JF Solutions applies structured root cause analysis, corrective action planning and effectiveness verification to help organizations address recurring process and quality issues.
