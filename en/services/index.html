How I work

Assessment, priorities, implementation and verification. Always in that order.

  1. Assessment

    I walk the plant, review records and listen to management and floor staff to understand how the operation really runs.

  2. Priorities

    I rank findings by risk and we agree together on what gets handled first and what can wait.

  3. Implementation

    Every action has an owner and a date. I support the changes to procedures, forms or training.

  4. Verification

    I go back to the floor at 30, 60 and 90 days to confirm with evidence that every action works.

Demonstration case

What a traceability project looks like, step by step

Hypothetical example for illustration only. It does not describe a real client or show actual results.

Situation
A mid-sized beverage plant has a customer audit in a few weeks, and its last mock recall could not locate all the finished product.
Assessment
A real lot is followed from receiving to shipping, and lot coding, packaging records and hold management are reviewed.
Priorities
The lot code turns out to change format between filling and the warehouse. Unifying it is prioritized before anything else.
Implementation
The coding procedure and shipping form are updated, both shifts are trained and each area gets an owner in a RACI matrix.
Verification
The exercise is repeated with another lot, and the floor is checked at 30, 60 and 90 days to confirm every shift uses the new format.
