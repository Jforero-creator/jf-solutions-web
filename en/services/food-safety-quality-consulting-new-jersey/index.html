Problems I solve

These are the problems I run into most often when I walk into a plant.

  • An audit is coming and there is no clear starting pointPlenty of open tasks, but no priority set by risk.
  • Repeat findingsThe same observations come back audit after audit because actions are closed without fixing the cause.
  • Records filled in at the end of the shiftThe forms exist, but they do not reflect what actually happened on the line.
  • Holds with no ownerProduct on hold piles up without an owner or a disposition date.
  • Management and the floor hear different thingsInstructions get lost between languages, shifts and levels.
  • KPIs nobody reviewsData gets collected but never turns into decisions.

Scope and deliverables

What is included

  • Floor walk-through of key areas and shifts: receiving, processing, packaging and warehouse.
  • Review of programs and records: HACCP plan or food safety plan, prerequisite programs, GMPs, cleaning and sanitation, allergens and traceability.
  • Interviews with management (strategic questionnaire) and floor staff (Gemba questions).
  • Findings classified by nature and risk.
  • Action plan with an owner, a due date and closing criteria for every item.
  • On-the-floor follow-up and verification at 30, 60 and 90 days.

What you get

  1. Findings report with evidence, ranked by risk.
  2. Prioritized action plan with owners and dates.
  3. Results meeting with management.
  4. Floor verification at 30/60/90 days with the status of every action.

Responsible scope. JF Solutions is not a certification or accreditation body, does not perform certification audits and does not guarantee the outcome of any audit or inspection. My job is to help you get your controls organized, documented and working on the floor.

How I work

Assessment, priorities, implementation and verification. Always in that order.

  1. Assessment

    I walk the plant, review records and listen to management and floor staff to understand how the operation really runs.

  2. Priorities

    I rank findings by risk and we agree together on what gets handled first and what can wait.

  3. Implementation

    Every action has an owner and a date. I support the changes to procedures, forms or training.

  4. Verification

    I go back to the floor at 30, 60 and 90 days to confirm with evidence that every action works.

Frequently asked questions

Do you only work with plants in New Jersey?

I am based in New Jersey and that is where I focus on-site work. If your operation is in another state, reach out and we will see whether the project makes sense.

Do I need an upcoming audit to hire you?

No. Many plants call me before an audit, but the assessment also helps set priorities, prepare for a process change or restart programs that slipped.

What do I need to prepare for the assessment?

Plant access, a point of contact, your written programs, recent records and any recent audit or inspection reports you want to share.

Do you issue certificates or guarantee audit results?

No. I am not a certification body and I do not guarantee audit results. I help you find gaps, fix them and verify that controls work.

Do you work in English and Spanish?

Yes. Reports, procedures and training can be delivered in either language, or both.

How much does it cost?

It depends on plant size and scope. The initial assessment is free: we use it to define the scope and I send you a written proposal.
