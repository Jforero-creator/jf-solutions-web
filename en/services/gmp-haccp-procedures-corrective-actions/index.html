Who I help

Plants that need to review their HACCP plan or food safety plan after a change in product, equipment or process.

Procedures nobody follows, duplicate versions or forms that no longer match the line.

Supervisors who need to train

New hires or high turnover that call for hands-on GMP training, in English or Spanish.

Problems I solve

What is usually breaking down when a plant asks me for this support.

  • SOPs that do not match the lineThe procedure says one thing, the operator does another, and neither is entirely wrong.
  • Incomplete CCP monitoringRecords with blanks, no verification, or no documented corrective action after a deviation.
  • CAPAs closed without a root causeActions that fix the symptom and get closed without checking whether they worked.
  • GMPs that slip between auditsHygiene, attire, allergen control or utensil handling that depend on who is supervising.
  • Staff who do not know the whyForms get filled out because they have to, without knowing which hazard they control.
  • Scattered documentationPlans, programs and records spread across folders, emails and different versions.

Scope and deliverables

What is included

  • Review of the HACCP plan or food safety plan against the real operation: flow diagram, hazard analysis, CCPs or preventive controls, limits, monitoring, corrective actions, verification and records.
  • Review of prerequisite programs and good manufacturing practices (GMPs).
  • Writing or updating procedures (SOPs) and record forms together with your team.
  • Nonconformance and CAPA system: logging, root cause analysis (5 Whys, fishbone), action plan and effectiveness check.
  • Hands-on training in GMPs, CCP monitoring and record keeping, in English or Spanish.

What you get

  1. Gap matrix for the plan and programs, with priorities.
  2. Updated procedures and forms (Word and Excel) under version control.
  3. Nonconformance and CAPA log with a closing workflow and effectiveness check.
  4. Training materials with attendance records and assessments.

HACCP and FSMA, stated precisely. Depending on your plant, the applicable framework may be HACCP (for example, in USDA-FSIS inspected plants or in juice and seafood) or the FDA FSMA food safety plan with preventive controls. I help you review, organize and update the documentation with your team; responsibility for the plan, its validation and its approval stays with your company and its designated person (for example, the PCQI or HACCP coordinator). I do not issue certifications, do not teach accredited courses and do not guarantee audit results.

How I work

Assessment, priorities, implementation and verification. Always in that order.

  1. Assessment

    I compare what is written with what happens on the line: plan, programs, records and floor practices.

  2. Priorities

    I rank the gaps by food safety risk and by what your customer or scheme requires.

  3. Implementation

    We update procedures and forms, launch the CAPA workflow and train the staff.

  4. Verification

    I review records and watch on the floor that the changes hold, and that every CAPA was closed with evidence of effectiveness.

Frequently asked questions

Can you write my HACCP plan?

I can develop or update it with you and your team based on your real process. I do not hand over generic templates: the plan has to reflect your plant, and its validation and approval belong to your designated person.

What is the difference between HACCP and a preventive controls plan?

Both start with a hazard analysis. HACCP focuses on critical control points and is mandatory in certain sectors; an FSMA food safety plan can also include allergen, sanitation and supply-chain controls. During the assessment we confirm which one applies to your plant.

Does the training come with a certificate?

I provide attendance records and an assessment for every session, useful as evidence of internal training. These are not accredited courses; if your customer or scheme requires one, I will tell you so you can take it with a recognized provider.

What does a properly closed corrective action look like?

One that identifies the root cause, fixes the problem, prevents recurrence and has a later check showing it worked, with a date and an owner.

Do you work with SQF, BRCGS or FSSC 22000?

I can help you organize the programs and records those schemes review and prepare your team. JF Solutions is not a certification body and does not audit for certification.
