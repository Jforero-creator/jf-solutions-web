Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains what data the jfsolutionsq.com website of JF Solutions collects, what it is used for, who it is shared with and how you can exercise your rights.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. We do not ask for sensitive data; please do not include it in your message.

Providers involved

  • Web3Forms: receives the form and forwards it to the JF Solutions inbox.
  • Google Workspace / Gmail: the inbox where requests are received and answered.
  • Vercel: site hosting, technical logs and cookieless statistics.
  • Google Analytics 4: analytics, only with your consent.
  • Google Fonts and Unsplash: fonts and images; your browser connects to their servers to download them.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), your email app, Instagram, YouTube and Fiverr: only if you choose to use them; their own policies apply.

Cookies and browser storage

Google Analytics uses cookies (_ga and similar) only if you accept. The site also stores in your browser, without sending it anywhere, your preferred language and your analytics choice. You can change your choice at any time: .

How long we keep data

Requests are kept as long as needed to answer them and follow up on the business relationship, and for at most 24 months after the last contact if you do not become a client. Google Analytics data is kept for the period configured in that tool.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, by writing to jforero@jfsolutions80.com. We reply within 45 days at most.

Children

This site is aimed at businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date shown above.
